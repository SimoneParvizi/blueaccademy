# Code of Conduct

blueaccademy should be a professional, respectful, and safe place to learn,
build, and collaborate.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient
- Give feedback on ideas and code without attacking people
- Assume good faith, but accept correction when you are wrong
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and useful to other contributors

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory or hateful language or behavior
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or deliberately bad-faith engagement
- Sexualized language, unwanted sexual attention, or inappropriate imagery
- Sharing someone else's private information without explicit permission

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits, review comments, Discord, and any other official blueaccademy
community space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or reject content that violates this Code
of Conduct. They may also warn, suspend, or ban participants depending on the
severity or pattern of behavior.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to
`parvizi.simone@gmail.com`.

All reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as promptly and
confidentially as reasonably possible.
