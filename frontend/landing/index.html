Question 14 of 32
A pod in namespace prod can't reach a ClusterIP service in monitoring. What do you check first?
Check NetworkPolicy rules in both namespaces. Run:
kubectl get netpol -n prod
kubectl get netpol -n monitoring
Verify ingress rules allow traffic from the prod namespace selector.

Hey — I can see you're working on the NetworkPolicy lab. What do you need help with?
My pods in the prod namespace can't reach the monitoring service. I already checked the service exists.
Good start. Next step: check if there's a NetworkPolicy in the monitoring namespace that restricts ingress. Run:

kubectl get netpol -n monitoring -o yaml

Look for ingress.from rules — they might not include a namespaceSelector matching prod.
Found it — the policy only allows from namespace "default". How do I add prod?
Add another entry under ingress.from:

- namespaceSelector:
    matchLabels:
      name: prod

Make sure the prod namespace has that label. Check with kubectl get ns prod --show-labels.
